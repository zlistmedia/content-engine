Questions people ask

Is the Content Engine Starter really free?

Yes. The plugin is free. You'll need a paid Claude plan (Pro or above), because the video editing runs in Claude's own workspace.

What do I need to film?

You, talking to camera for five to ten minutes, on your phone. Stumble, start again, go off on a tangent. Claude finds the retakes and cuts them.

Which platforms are the Reels for?

Instagram, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Each Reel is captioned word by word in your colours and fonts, with a caption written for each platform.

How long does it take?

About fifteen minutes from a five to ten minute video. Transcribing takes about a third of your video's length, then each short Reel takes about two minutes and the 60-second story about four. Setting up your Brand Card is a one-off, about fifteen minutes.

What if my video is longer than 15 minutes?

It still works. A longer video just takes Claude a little longer to work through.

What's the difference between the Starter and Boss Level?

The Starter turns your video into three short Reels and a 60-second story cut. Boss Level takes the same video and also makes the YouTube edit, two thumbnails, the upload copy, a blog post, an email, two Pinterest pins, six Threads posts and an Instagram carousel, all built for search. It's one payment of $197.
