What do I get for $497?

From every long recording it makes a transcript, a captions file and chapters, 10 to 15 Reels, a preview cut, two blog posts, three emails, four Pinterest pins, ten Threads posts and two carousels, plus a Ready to Post folder with every post planned. Everything in Boss Level is included too, for your weekly videos, and every update comes to your inbox.

How long does a run take?

About an hour for a one-hour recording. The transcript takes about four minutes, each Reel one to two minutes, and the writing about twenty. Claude works while you get on with your day and stops only for your four check-ins.

Can I use audio only, like a podcast?

Yes. Audio-only episodes get podcast-style Reels with your photo on a card in your colours. Zoom recordings, screen shares and slide-heavy lessons work the same way.

What about two hosts?

Two people side by side get the split-screen look, with one above the other and your hook in a band at the top. If you record in separate tracks, use your tool's combined side-by-side export.

Will it give my paid course away?

No. Each post teases one idea and points back to the course. Claude skips housekeeping, student names and anything you'd rather keep inside, and you approve every clip before it's made.

How do I get a big recording to Claude?

Share it from Google Drive with a link, or attach the audio file and a photo of you if the video is too big. An hour of audio is small enough to attach straight to the chat.

Is it a subscription?

No. It's one payment. You'll also need your own paid Claude plan (Pro or above), because the editing runs in Claude's own workspace.

I already have Boss Level. What happens?

Remove Boss Level in Claude and add this one. Your brand setup carries straight over, and everything Boss Level does is still there.
