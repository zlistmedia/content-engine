# How to give notes (send once, the first time)

You can't break anything. Say what you'd say to an editor, in your own words. A few examples that
work well:

- "Short 1: start at 'Here's what I found' instead."
- "Short 2 at 0:22, cut the 'um'."
- "Short 3 ends too early, keep the next sentence."
- "Make the captions bigger."
- "Hook for Short 1: NOBODY FOUND ME ON INSTAGRAM."
- "Music quieter." / "No music on Short 3."
- "That's not my yellow, it's #F5D547."

Times help (pause the video and read the time), but "near the end" is fine too.
Only what you mention gets changed, and only those Shorts get sent back.
