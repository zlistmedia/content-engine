# Scheduling: helping the owner set it up on their own platforms

The engine doesn't post for them. It hands over the Ready to Post folder, then helps them schedule
in whatever they already use. Menus change often: these guides were checked in October 2026. If a
button has moved, say so plainly and help them find it (Claude in Chrome can look at the page with
them if it's connected).

## The first time: ask, then walk through it

Ask one question: "Where do you schedule your posts now? Or do you post straight from your phone?"
Then open the matching guide and take them through it one step at a time, waiting for "done"
between steps. Don't paste the whole guide at once.

| They say | Guide |
|---|---|
| Instagram app, Facebook app, "I just post from my phone" | `instagram-facebook.md` (app section) |
| Meta Business Suite, Facebook Business, Meta planner | `instagram-facebook.md` (Business Suite section) |
| YouTube, YouTube Studio | `youtube-shorts.md` |
| TikTok | `tiktok.md` |
| Buffer, Later, Metricool, Planoly, Hootsuite, Hopper, SocialBee, Publer | `schedulers.md` |
| GoHighLevel, Tekmatix, HighLevel, LeadConnector | `gohighlevel.md` |
| "Nothing", "I don't know" | Recommend the free native route: the Instagram app's own scheduler for Reels (and Facebook via Business Suite), YouTube Studio for Shorts, TikTok Studio on a computer. No new tool to learn. |

Read `platforms.scheduler` in the profile first: setup usually asked already. Only ask if it's
empty, then save their answer there so you never ask again.

## Every week after that

The Posting plan page in the folder lists each post with its time and captions and a Copy button.
The rhythm for one video's three Shorts:

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Day 1 (the day the main video goes out, or any day they choose) | Short 1 |
| Day 3 | Short 2 |
| Day 5 or 6 | Short 3 |

Use their own posting times if they have them. Otherwise suggest 7.30 to 8pm local time, at odd
minutes (7.38pm, not 7.30pm).

## Prompts they can copy (put these in the Posting plan or the reply when useful)

- "Open Meta Business Suite with me and help me schedule the three Shorts in my Ready to Post folder."
- "I'm stuck on the YouTube Studio Visibility step. What do I click?"
- "Make my schedule.csv match Buffer's bulk upload format."
- "Move Short 2 to Friday and give me the new caption file."
