# TikTok

Checked October 2026. Menu names can shift.

TikTok's own scheduler works on a computer, not in the phone app. It needs a Creator or Business
account (in the app: Settings and privacy > Account > Switch to Business Account, or check
Creator tools).

## On a computer (TikTok Studio)

1. Go to tiktok.com, log in, and open TikTok Studio.
2. Click Upload and choose the Short from the Ready to Post folder.
3. Paste the caption from `TikTok caption.txt`.
4. Pick the cover frame.
5. Turn on Schedule, choose the date and time, then click Schedule.

TikTok uses the computer's time zone and schedules up to 10 days ahead. A scheduled post can't be
edited: delete it and upload again.

## From a phone

Post at the planned time from the app (+ > Upload), or use one of the schedulers in `schedulers.md`.
Set a phone reminder for the time on the Posting plan.

## Common snags

- No Schedule toggle: the account is personal. Switch it to Business or Creator.
- Captions look doubled: TikTok's auto-captions are on. The Short already has captions burned in,
  so turn auto-captions off in the post settings.
