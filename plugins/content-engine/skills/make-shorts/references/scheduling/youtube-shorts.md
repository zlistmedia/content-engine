# YouTube Shorts

Checked October 2026. Menu names can shift.

Scheduling needs a verified channel (a one-off phone check at youtube.com/verify).

## On a computer (YouTube Studio)

1. Go to studio.youtube.com.
2. Click Create > Upload videos and choose the Short from the Ready to Post folder. Vertical videos
   under three minutes are recognised as Shorts automatically.
3. Paste the title and description from `YouTube Shorts caption.txt`.
4. Audience: choose "No, it's not made for kids" (unless it is).
5. Click Next until you reach Visibility.
6. Choose Schedule, set the date and time, click Schedule.

To check: Content > Shorts, where a scheduled one shows a calendar icon.

## On a phone (YouTube app)

1. Save the Short to the phone.
2. In the YouTube app tap + > Upload a video, and pick it.
3. Tap Next, paste the title and description.
4. Under Visibility, choose Schedule, set the time, then tap Upload or Schedule.

## Common snags

- No Schedule option: the channel isn't verified yet (youtube.com/verify).
- It uploaded as a normal video, not a Short: the file must be vertical. The engine's files are
  1080x1920, so check they picked the Short and not another file.
- A link in the description isn't clickable: Shorts descriptions don't show clickable links in the
  feed. Put the link in a pinned comment as well, once the Short is live.
