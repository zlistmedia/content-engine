# Check your Shorts on your phone

Send this with the first Shorts they review (once, not every time). Watch each Short on your phone,
with sound, the way your followers will.

1. **The first second.** Does it start cleanly, on a word, with nothing cut off?
2. **The last second.** Does your last word finish, with a beat after it?
3. **Captions.** Is every word right, especially names? Can you read them easily?
4. **The hook.** Does the top line make you want to watch?
5. **You.** Are you in frame the whole time, not cut off at the side?
6. **Sound.** Is your voice clear and is the music low enough?
7. **Colours and fonts.** Does it look like your brand?

Reply with anything that's off, by Short number and time if you can:
"Short 2 at 0:14, the caption says 'sells' but I said 'cells'."
