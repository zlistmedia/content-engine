# The Brand Card

Every owner fills in the Brand Card before setup. It's one page with six short sections: business,
colours, fonts, Shorts look, voice, and offers and posting. A live preview shows their hook and
captions in their own colours and fonts as they go. It also checks every colour pair is easy to
read and flags anything missing.

**The page:** https://claude.ai/artifact/A3mYjSvnNzWAyv9Ysu7735

The Brand Card link has to be shared publicly (anyone with the link can open it), or buyers can't
open it. If an owner says the link asks them to sign in or says they don't have access, give them
the document route below instead and suggest they let Z List Media know.

What to tell an owner who hasn't done it yet:

> Before we set up, fill in your Brand Card. It's one page and takes about 10 minutes:
> <link>
> Have your colour codes and font files handy (the page tells you where to find them). When you're
> done, tap "Save my brand card" and attach the file here. If saving doesn't work on your device,
> tap "Copy it for Claude", paste it here, and attach your font files.

What comes back:

- **The zip** (`<business>-brand-card.zip`):
  - `Brand/brand-profile.json`, already in the engine's profile format
  - `Brand/fonts/` with their font files
  - `Brand/Brand card.html`, a one-page summary they can keep
- **Or the pasted text:** the same profile as JSON, with font files attached separately. Save it as
  `Brand/brand-profile.json`, put the fonts in `Brand/fonts/` with the file names the profile
  lists, and fix any path that doesn't match.

No web page possible (the link won't open, an offline owner, or someone who'd rather fill in a
document): `style-guide-template.md` asks for the same things as a Word or Google document to fill
in and attach. Send it as a file they can open in Word, or paste it into the chat for them to copy.
