# Content Engine by Z List Media

One video of you talking to camera goes in. Three Shorts come out, captioned in your colours, your fonts and your words, with a caption for each platform and a folder that tells you what to post where and when.

## Add it to Claude

1. In Claude, open **Customize**, then **Plugins**.
2. Choose **Add marketplace** and paste `zlistmedia/content-engine`.
3. Install **Content Engine**, start a new chat and say "set up my content engine".

You'll need a paid Claude plan (Pro or above) and your Brand Card. Get both steps and the Brand Card from [zlistmedia.com.au/content-engine](https://zlistmedia.com.au/content-engine).
